🎮 青年閣使用守則

歡迎預約青年閣！這裡是屬於大家的放鬆空間（Chill Zone）。為了讓每位 12-24 歲的朋友都能玩得開心，預約前請先閱讀並同意以下「四大守則」：

  1. 準時出席，請勿無故缺席 ✈️
    • 時段保留：預約成功後請準時到達。如果遲到超過 15 分鐘且沒有提前通知，該時段將會開放給現場其他朋友。
    • 提前取消：如需取消預約，請至少在 2 小時前於系統取消，切勿浪費名額。
  2. 愛護物資，共享快樂 🎱
    • 愛惜裝備：請勿過度用力按壓 PS5 手掣，桌球桿與康樂棋請勿用作嬉戲碰撞。桌遊玩完後請清點好組件並放回原位。
    • 主動申報：若發現物資損壞，請立即通知社工，切勿隱瞞，以免影響下一位使用者。
  3. 保持整潔，共同維護 🥤
    • 垃圾清理：青年閣歡迎大家帶飲品和小食，但享用後請自覺將垃圾丟進垃圾桶，保持環境乾淨。
    • 一起收拾：每節結束前 5 分鐘，請大家一起動手收拾好物資，方便下一節的朋友入座。
  4. 互相尊重，和諧相處 🤝
    • 禮貌互動：請保持禮貌，請勿大聲喧嘩、說粗言穢語、打架或欺凌。打機勝負是平常事，最重要是玩得開心！
    • 聽從指示：請配合現場社工或職員的指示與安排。

重要提示：公物價值參考（請愛惜公物 💸）

若因不當使用而導致故意破壞或遺失公物，必須照價賠償。價值參考如下：

  • 🎱 桌球（整副）：$12,000 （特別注意：桌球若遺失任何一粒，均需整副賠償！）
  • 🎮 PS5 主機及配件：$5,000
  • 🪵 康樂棋：$4,000
  • 🃏 桌遊（Boardgames）：每副約 $300 - $500 （視乎款式）

🎮 Youth Corner Rules

Welcome to the Youth Corner! This is a Chill Zone for everyone. To ensure all friends aged 12-24 have a great time, please read and agree to the following 4 Simple Rules before confirming your booking:

  1. Be Punctual, Don't Be a Ghost ✈️
    • Booking Retention: Please arrive on time. If you are late for more than 15 minutes without notice, your slot will be released to other users.
    • Cancellation: If you need to cancel, please do so via the system at least 2 hours in advance. Don't waste the slot!
  2. Care for Our Equipment 🎱
    • Gentle Use: Please handle PS5 controllers with care (don't smash the buttons!). Do not play roughly with pool cues or carrom sticks. Please count all pieces and put boardgames back in place after playing.
    • Report Damage: If you find anything broken, please tell our social workers immediately. Do not hide it!
  3. Keep It Clean and Tidy 🥤
    • Clean Up: Food and drinks are welcome, but please throw away your trash in the bin to keep the area clean.
    • Pack Up Together: Please spend the last 5 minutes of your session packing up, so the next group can enjoy it.
  4. Respect Each Other (Good Vibes Only) 🤝
    • Be Polite: No shouting, swearing, fighting, or bullying. Winning or losing a game is secondary—having fun is what matters!
    • Follow Staff Guidance: Please cooperate with our social workers and staff on-site.

Notice: Equipment Compensation List 💸

If any property is damaged or lost due to improper use, full compensation is required. Reference prices:

  • 🎱 Billiards (Full Set): $12,000 (Important Note: If any single ball is lost, compensation for the entire set is required!)
  • 🎮 PS5 Console & Accessories: $5,000
  • 🪵 Carrom Board: $4,000
  • 🃏 Boardgames: Around $300 - $500 per set (Depends on the game)
